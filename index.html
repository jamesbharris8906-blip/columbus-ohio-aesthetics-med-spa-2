Botox

Botox is a purified protein that relaxes the small muscles that crease the skin. It is used most often on forehead lines, the lines between the brows, and crow's feet at the corners of the eyes. A few small injections take about fifteen minutes, and most people go back to work the same day. The result appears over a few days, and it usually lasts three to four months. A Columbus Ohio Aesthetics Med Spa visit for Botox starts with a talk, not a needle: the provider will ask what bothers you, watch how your face moves, and mark the spots to treat.

A light touch matters here: too much Botox can leave a face looking flat, while the right amount softens lines and keeps your expressions your own. Ask to see photos of past work, ask how many units are planned and why, and ask what to do if a brow feels heavy after. A good provider will answer all three without rushing you.

Cheek Filler

Cheek Filler restores volume to cheeks that have flattened with age. A filler placed deep on the cheekbone can lift the mid-face, soften the lines around the nose and mouth, and give the face a rested look. The result is seen at once, and it usually lasts a year or more. Swelling for a day or two is common, and bruising can happen, so plan a first visit when you have a free day after.

Cheek filler is one of the treatments where skill shows most. The cheek has blood vessels that must be avoided, and the filler must sit at the right depth to look smooth. A provider who knows the anatomy of the face will talk about the risks, use a cannula when it is safer, and keep the amount modest at first. You can always add more later.

Facial Balancing

Facial Balancing looks at the whole face, not one feature at a time. The provider checks how the chin, cheeks, jaw, and lips sit together, and places small amounts of filler where balance is missing. A weak chin can make a nose look larger. Flat cheeks can make the lower face look heavy. A little filler in the right spot can bring the face into proportion without changing what makes it yours.

This is a plan, not a single visit. The first visit sets the map: photos, a talk about what bothers you, and a list of areas to treat in order. Some areas are treated at once, while others wait for a later visit. The result builds over weeks, and the provider will check the balance at each step. Ask how the plan is priced, what is treated first and why, and when the next check is.

Lip Filler

Lip Filler adds shape and volume to lips that have thinned with age, or that have always been thin. A hyaluronic acid filler is placed with a fine needle or a cannula, and the result is seen at once. The lips may swell for a day or two, and the final shape settles over about two weeks. Most fillers last six to twelve months.

The aim is balance, not size. A skilled provider will look at your whole face, not just your lips, and will talk about the shape that suits your features. Ask how much is planned, what product is used, and how the filler can be dissolved if you do not like the result. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved, and knowing that takes the fear out of a first visit.

Microneedling

Microneedling uses a device with fine needles to make tiny, controlled openings in the skin. The skin answers by making new collagen, the protein that keeps skin firm and smooth. It is used for fine lines, acne scars, large pores, and skin that looks tired. A numbing cream is put on first, so most people feel pressure more than pain. The skin may look pink for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, and it settles fast.

A set of three treatments, spaced a few weeks apart, is the usual plan. One visit can help, but the change builds with each one. After each visit, the skin needs gentle care: no harsh scrubs, no heavy makeup for a day, and sunscreen every day. A Columbus Ohio Aesthetics Med Spa provider may pair microneedling with a light peel, or space the two a few weeks apart, so the skin heals well from each.

Diamond Glow Facial

Diamond Glow Facial is a skin treatment that cleans, exfoliates, and infuses serum in one visit. A diamond-tipped wand sweeps away dead skin, opens pores, and lets a serum sink in while the skin is fresh. It suits people who want a glow before an event, or a regular reset for dull skin. There is little downtime, and makeup can usually go on the same day.

The treatment is gentle enough for most skin types, and the serum can be chosen for the skin in front of the provider: hydration for dry skin, brightening for dark spots, or calming for redness. A single facial gives a fresh look for a week or so, while a set of facials builds a steadier glow. Ask which serum is planned for your skin, and what to use at home between visits.

Chemical Peels

Chemical Peels use a solution to lift the top layers of skin, so fresher skin shows through. Light peels brighten the skin and smooth fine lines, with a few days of flaking. Medium peels reach deeper, and they help with dark spots and acne scars, with a longer recovery. The provider will choose the peel that fits your skin tone, your goals, and the time you have to heal.

Peels need care after. Sunscreen is a must, because new skin burns easily, and picking at peeling skin can scar it. A good provider will tell you what to expect day by day, what to use and what to avoid, and when to come back. People with darker skin tones should ask about the risk of dark marks, and about peels that are safer for their skin. A careful plan matters more than a strong peel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Botox last?

Most people see results for three to four months. The lines come back slowly, and a repeat visit keeps the result steady.

Does lip filler hurt?

A numbing cream is used first, and most fillers contain a numbing agent too. Most people feel pressure and a few small pinches, and the lips may be sore for a day.

How many microneedling visits do I need?

A set of three visits, spaced a few weeks apart, is the usual plan. One visit can help, but the change builds with each one.

What is a Diamond Glow Facial good for?

It cleans, exfoliates, and infuses serum in one visit. It suits dull skin, dry skin, and anyone who wants a glow before an event, with little downtime.

Can I get a chemical peel in summer?

Yes, but sun care matters more. New skin burns easily, so sunscreen, a hat, and shade are a must after a peel, in any season.

How do I choose between Botox and filler?

Botox relaxes muscles that crease the skin, while filler adds volume where it has been lost. Many people need both, in different areas, and a Columbus Ohio Aesthetics Med Spa provider will map the plan with you.
